Data Analyst - Bengaluru

About the role
We are hiring a Data Analyst to turn business data into clear insights for our product and sales teams.

Responsibilities
- Clean and analyze large datasets using Python, SQL and Excel
- Build interactive dashboards in Power BI or Tableau
- Perform exploratory data analysis and present findings to stakeholders
- Apply statistics to find trends and support business decisions
- Automate recurring reports and maintain data pipelines

Requirements
- Strong SQL and Python (Pandas, NumPy) skills
- Hands-on experience with Power BI, including data visualization and reporting
- Good knowledge of statistics and data analysis
- Good communication and team collaboration skills

Nice to have
- Experience with ETL or data pipelines
- Familiarity with Git and basic cloud tools such as AWS
