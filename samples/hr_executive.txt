HR Executive - Bengaluru

About the role
We are hiring an HR Executive to support recruitment, onboarding and employee engagement.

Responsibilities
- Source candidates through job portals and LinkedIn, and schedule interviews
- Conduct onboarding and maintain employee records
- Handle payroll inputs, attendance and leave management
- Organize employee engagement activities and training sessions
- Ensure compliance with company policies and labor laws
- Resolve employee queries and support performance reviews

Requirements
- Bachelor's or Master's degree in HR, Business or a related field
- Strong communication and interpersonal skills
- Knowledge of recruitment, payroll and HR policies
- Proficiency in Microsoft Excel
- Good time management and team collaboration skills
