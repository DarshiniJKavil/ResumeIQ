Marketing Executive - Bengaluru

About the role
We are looking for a creative Marketing Executive to plan and run campaigns that grow our brand and customer base.

Responsibilities

Requirements
- Bachelor's degree in Marketing, Business or a related field
- Excellent written and verbal communication skills
- Experience with content writing and social media management
- Knowledge of SEO, email marketing and Canva
- Good time management and team collaboration skills
